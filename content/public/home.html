More questions and answers {{icon:arrow}}
How do I earn points?

Discover an eligible game through Bonus Round, review its offer, and complete the qualifying gameplay activities. The offer explains which activities earn points and any deadlines or restrictions.

What can I redeem my points for?

You can use enough approved points to request an available gift card. Check the app’s rewards catalog for current brands, point requirements and redemption conditions. Points have no cash value.

How long will my gift card take?

Processing and delivery details will be shown when you redeem. Timing can depend on verification and the gift card provider. Contact support if a reward is taking longer than the time shown for your request.

Who can use Bonus Round?

Bonus Round is for eligible adults age 18 and older in supported locations. Country availability and device requirements will be confirmed before launch. See eligibility details.
