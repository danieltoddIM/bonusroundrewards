Essential storage only by default.

This website does not currently load analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, embedded social media, or optional third-party tracking scripts. It uses browser storage to remember the privacy choices you make here.

The preference entry is named bonusround_privacy_v1. It contains your cookie categories and the time you saved them, not your account identifier or the text you type into a contact form. Choices expire after 180 days. You can clear them sooner in your browser.

If your browser blocks storage, the site still works. Your choice may apply only to the current visit and the banner may return on another page or visit.

Optional categories.

The preference controls include analytics and marketing categories. Both start off. No service in either category is installed in this version of the website, so accepting optional cookies does not begin tracking.

If optional services are introduced, this page and the Privacy Policy must describe them, and those services must respect your consent and any applicable opt-out signal before loading. Your choice here applies to this website in this browser; it does not change your app account’s settings.

Global Privacy Control.

When your browser exposes an enabled Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal to this website, optional analytics and marketing preferences stay off. We do not require you to turn GPC off.

Optional services are off by default. Browser signal detection requires JavaScript.

This browser-level control does not identify your Bonus Round account. For a request covering app or account information, use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page or contact {{privacyEmail}}.

Requests needed to deliver the website.

Your browser sends ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and browser headers, to our hosting provider to load the site. Hosting and security logging are separate from optional advertising cookies. The confirmed provider, retention and purposes must be documented in the final Privacy Policy.
