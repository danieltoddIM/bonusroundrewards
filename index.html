01 / Strategic fit

Game-show fans already play along at home.

Scrabble and Trivial Pursuit viewers spend the show guessing words, racing the clock, and testing recall — the same instincts that make casual word, trivia, and puzzle games work as a mobile habit. Bonus Round turns that existing behavior into a daily reason to open an app, not a new one to teach.

Show-native instincts

No new behavior to teach

Two shows, one shared app

A single rewards platform serves both fan bases, with a distinct partner placement for each show.
