Calculation details

QR installs = total annual viewer exposures × scan rate × scan-to-install rate. Social installs = network promotional waves × 7,808,100 followers × reach rate × click rate × store conversion. Net annual installs = (QR installs + social installs) × (1 − duplicate rate).

The base case assumes 50 episodes per show, no additional rerun inventory, and 52 social promotional waves across the CW accounts. The number of waves and participating channels remain subject to agreement. Results are planning scenarios, not forecasts or guarantees.
