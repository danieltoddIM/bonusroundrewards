Assumptions and methodology

Starts with zero active players. Net annual installs are spread evenly over 12 months and repeated in year 2. Monthly new engaged players = annual installs ÷ 12 × engagement rate. Each month’s active base = previous base × (1 − churn) + new engaged players.

Revenue uses each month’s active base. Year 2 totals sum months 13–24. The month-24 run rate multiplies that month by 12. Steady state = monthly new engaged players ÷ churn; with positive inflow and zero churn there is no finite steady state.

The $5.50 gross rate and $2.00–$2.25 partner-share range are Influence Mobile planning inputs. Reward and operating costs are not separately modeled. These estimates are not forecasts or guarantees. The 200,000-player illustration on the overview is a separate scale scenario.
