A little play. A little progress. A reward you choose.

From finding a game to choosing a gift card, here’s how your next round works.

01

Find a game that looks like fun.

Open Bonus Round and explore the games available to your account. Before you start, check the offer for its qualifying activities, points, deadlines and device requirements.

  • Begin the offer through Bonus Round so eligible activity can be attributed to your account.
  • Some offers may require a first-time install or a new game account. Check before installing.
  • Game availability can change and may differ by location or device.
02

Play. Make progress. Earn points.

Complete the eligible activities in the offer. Your points depend on the activity and its requirements; time spent playing by itself does not always qualify.

  • Keep track of goals and deadlines in the app.
  • Allow time for activity to be reported and verified.
  • If something does not appear, save the offer details and contact support.
03

Choose a gift card for you.

When you have enough approved points, open the rewards catalog, choose an available gift card and follow the redemption steps.

  • Review the point requirement, gift card amount and any limits before confirming.
  • Make sure the account and delivery details you provide are correct.
  • Gift cards have their own issuer terms. Points are not cash and cannot be transferred or sold.

Make room for a bonus round.

Check the app for current bonus opportunities and the rules that apply to each one.

Daily streaks

A streak may require a qualifying activity on each eligible day. Opening the app alone may not count. Check the reset time and the effect of a missed day.

Bonus events

Events may add a limited-time goal or a bonus for eligible play. Read the start and end times, requirements and any exclusions before joining.

Clear offer rules

The current in-app offer controls the points you can earn. Screenshots on this website show examples, not guaranteed point amounts.
