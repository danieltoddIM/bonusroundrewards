Send a privacy-choice request

Email privacy@bonusroundrewards.com with the subject Do not sell or share my personal information. Say whether your request relates to the Bonus Round app, this website, or both.

If you want the request applied to an app account, include the account email or player ID needed to locate it. Do not include passwords or identity documents. An opt-out request does not require identity verification. We may ask only for the information needed to locate the records or understand the choice.

Open privacy-choice email

This button opens a draft in your email app. The request is submitted only when you send it. If no email app opens, copy privacy@bonusroundrewards.com into your email service.

Authorized agents may submit a request where permitted. We may ask for evidence that the agent is authorized, consistent with applicable law. We will not require an account simply to exercise a privacy right.

Your browser and the app are separate

Optional analytics and advertising scripts are not enabled on this website. The Cookie settings control in the footer lets you review the preference stored in this browser.

Global Privacy Control (GPC) is a browser signal that communicates an opt-out preference. When recognized here, it keeps optional tracking disabled for this browser. The website does not sign you into Bonus Round or identify your app account. A browser preference therefore does not by itself update the app account or instruct the app's providers. Use the email option above for an account-related request.

If you clear browser storage or change devices, a locally saved choice may no longer be available. A supported GPC signal can continue to communicate your browser's preference.

What happens next

We will handle a received request within the period required by applicable law. Opting out does not delete your account. It also does not prevent processing that remains necessary and lawful to provide a service you request, fulfill a reward, or protect against fraud.
