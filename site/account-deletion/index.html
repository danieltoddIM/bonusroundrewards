You can request deletion of your Bonus Round account and its associated personal information. You do not need to reinstall the app to send a request by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bonus Round and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to [confirmed in-app deletion path].
  3. Review what will happen to your account, points, and pending redemptions, then confirm the deletion request.
  4. Follow any account-verification instructions and check the confirmation for the expected completion date.

Account deletion is different from signing out, deactivating an account, or uninstalling the app.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to privacy@bonusroundrewards.com with the subject Delete my Bonus Round account.

Include the email address associated with your account, or your player ID if you have one, and say that you want the account and associated personal information deleted. If possible, send the message from your account email. If you cannot access it, explain that in the request so support can help with another verification method.

Open deletion email

This link opens your email app. You must send the email to submit the request. If the link does not open, copy privacy@bonusroundrewards.com into your preferred email service. Do not include a password, full gift card code, or identity document in the initial message.
