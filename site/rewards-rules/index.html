1. The Bonus Round program

Bonus Round lets eligible users discover games, earn points through qualifying gameplay, and request available gift cards. The program is operated by [Bonus Round Rewards LLC], an Arizona limited liability company.

These rules supplement the Terms of Use. Each offer's displayed conditions determine which actions qualify for that offer. This page does not establish a sweepstakes, lottery, or prize drawing. Any future promotion that uses a drawing requires its own official rules.

2. Who can participate

3. Earning points

  1. Open an available offer in Bonus Round and read its conditions.
  2. Start or install the game through the offer when required. Confirm new-player eligibility before beginning.
  3. Complete the specified action within the stated time and other limits.
  4. Allow the activity to be verified and credited to your account.

Not every game session, installation, level, or purchase earns points. Activity must qualify under the offer and be attributed to the eligible account. Device settings, prior installs, incomplete conditions, or provider reporting delays may prevent or delay credit.

Point-credit timing and missing-credit reporting period: [confirmed timing and reporting window].

If credit appears to be missing, contact support with the game name, offer, completion date, and relevant screenshots. Do not submit passwords or full payment details.

5. Point values and examples

The app must show the actual points required for a reward before you request it. Points are not money and have no cash value. They cannot be sold, transferred, or redeemed for cash except where required by law or expressly permitted by the program.

7. Fraud prevention and review

Artificial activity, falsified identity or location, automated gameplay, duplicate-account abuse, manipulated attribution, and attempts to bypass offer limits are prohibited. VPNs, proxies, and emulators may not be used to evade eligibility or fraud checks. See the fair-play terms for the full draft rule.

Points linked to ineligible or fraudulent activity may be withheld or corrected, and an affected redemption may be paused during review. Contact support if you believe a decision is wrong.
