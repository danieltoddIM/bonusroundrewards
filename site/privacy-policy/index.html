Information We May Collect and How We Collect It

We may collect various types of information, including information you provide or make available to us directly, information collected from your devices (including web and mobile), and information collected from other sources, like social networking sites and gaming partners. We have provided more details and examples of each category of information, below.

Some of this information may be considered "personal information" or "personal data" under applicable privacy or data protection laws. As used herein, the terms "personal information" or "personal data" generally refer to information that identifies, relates to, describes, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked with a particular data subject, consumer, or household.

Information You Provide

You may provide personal information directly to Bonus Round Rewards when you:

  • Use or access the Services;
  • Communicate with or contact others using our Services, including when you use "invite a friend," or other similar features;
  • Contact us, including in writing for customer or technical support; or
  • Complete surveys offered by us or on our behalf.

In order to register an account, you must provide us with a first and last name and a phone number. Depending on how you choose to use the Services, you may also provide us with the following personal information:

  • Your email address, phone number, home address or shipping address, and other contact information; and
  • Your age, date of birth, and gender.

If you opt-in to one of our surveys, you may choose to provide us with the following:

  • Information about your health;
  • Information about your race and ethnicity;
  • Information about your employment;
  • Information about your education;
  • Information about your household; and/or
  • Information about your shopping habits.

Information Collected or Generated When You Use our Services

When you access or use our Services, we collect information from and about you and the device(s) you use. This may include the following information:

  • IP address, unique device identifiers (Device IDs), advertising identifiers (Ad IDs), device serial numbers, and similar identifiers (other IDs);
  • Hardware model, device software platform/OS and firmware, mobile carrier, preferred languages, and mobile network information, and in some cases, browser type, Internet service provider, referring/exit pages and URLs, or clickstream data;
  • App activity data, including which third party apps you have installed and data related to the use of third party apps installed via our Services;
  • Approximate geolocation data, zip/postal code (inferred from your IP address, using publicly available data);
  • Scores and achievements, points accrued, and other information as reasonably required to offer and enhance our Services; and/or
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and other app performance data.

When contacting our customer support you may be asked to provide us with photos, videos, voice or sound recordings in order to assist in troubleshooting technical issues. In that case, you give us permission to read your mobile phone's external storage.

Information Collected Using Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, beacons, software development kits, mobile analytics software, and/or similar technologies (which we refer to in this Privacy Policy under the general term "cookies") for a number of purposes, including to authenticate users, prevent fraud, remember preferences and settings, analyze advertising and messaging campaigns, analyze traffic and trends, personalize content (including ads), and otherwise provide the Services. These technologies may collect information about your use of our Services, pages you view, links you tap, third party applications you access, messages you open, and other actions taken in connection with our Services, as well the information referred to above, under the sub-heading "Information Collected or Generated When You Use our Services".

Depending on the choices and settings available for the browser(s) you use, you may be able to disable cookies in your browser settings. If you disable cookies or refuse consent, the availability and functionality of our Services may be impacted. For information on interest-based advertising and how to opt out, see the Interest-Based Advertising section below.

Information Collected From Other Sources

We may also collect information about your approximate location (including your zip/postal code, which we infer from your IP address), from other sources, including from our third-party partners or service providers and publicly available sources. We may also receive feedback from other players, or social networking sites as described below. We may combine this information with other information in our possession, which helps us to update, enhance, and analyze our Services, and provide tailored experiences for you.

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect for a number of business and commercial purposes, including to:

  • Internal operations: Perform internal operations necessary to develop, provide, maintain, and improve our Services, including troubleshooting, providing customer and technical support, and conducting data analysis, testing, and research.
  • Business model: To support our business model, which requires us to track third party app installations (conversions).
  • Core in-app Services: Perform our core in-app Services, including awarding points and rewards to users based on their active usage of our third party partner applications and enabling in-app gift card redemption.
  • Personalization: Understand how you interact with our Services and personalize the product experience and content (including advertising and messaging campaigns and tailoring third party app recommendations to you based on your location, your gender and age, and your mobile device's operating system).
  • Security and integrity: Maintain the safety, security, and integrity of our Services (and monitor compliance with our Terms of Use), for example by authenticating users (including via SMS/text verification codes used to confirm your phone number, which are sent for this security purpose and are not marketing communications) and providing account security and fraud detection.
  • Facilitation of social connections: Facilitate your communication with friends and other users, and enable features that allow you to share information with other people, including via social media and other third party platforms (for example, if you choose to use our invite a friend feature or embedded sharing options).
  • Legal: Comply with legal requirements and/or to investigate or address claims or disputes relating to your use of the Services.
  • Marketing: Market our Services (including any new features, promotions or offers, as well as any existing, new or related Apps) to potential and existing users, including in-app messaging, SMS, text, and push notifications.

We may share the information we collect about you with a variety of third parties in various ways, including:

  • With Influence Mobile and its affiliates, which operate the Services on our behalf, and with vendors, business partners, and other third-party service providers as necessary to carry out work on our behalf and otherwise support our provision of the Services, including vendors that provide us with IT infrastructure and software, professional services (such as legal, tax and accounting), marketing or promotional strategy, and analytics tools;
  • With third parties that help us provide our messaging services (specifically, Amazon Web Services (AWS) for account verification and transactional messages, and Klaviyo for promotional and marketing messages), as well as other platform providers, phone companies, and vendors who assist us in the delivery of SMS/text messages;
  • With our game developer business partners, for the purpose of establishing appropriate conversion rates based on your app installations;
  • With other players, to enable you to interact with them, including when you use "invite a friend," or other similar features, and to share App-related data such as gift card wins and other leaderboard information;
  • With the general public, if you choose to share information when contributing content to our Services through a forum, blogs, social features, or the like (to the extent such opportunities may be made available, from time to time);
  • With others who share your device, if you make your account available to them through that device;
  • With specific third parties if we believe disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process;
  • With law enforcement officials, government authorities, or other third parties if we believe your actions are inconsistent with our terms or other policies, and/or are fraudulent or in violation of applicable law, or as we determine necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Bonus Round Rewards or others;
  • In connection with a merger, consolidation, restructuring, sale of company stock and/or assets or other corporate change, including during the course of any due diligence process; and/or
  • In an aggregated form or otherwise in a form that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

We will not share your SMS/text messaging opt-in data or consent status with any third party for purposes unrelated to providing you with the services of the applicable messaging program. This protection applies to all users, regardless of state of residence.

Interest-Based Advertising

We may work with third-party advertising partners in the event that we serve third party advertisements within our Services. In such case, we may share information about you (including your approximate location, advertising identifiers, gender and age) with these partners to make advertising more relevant, measure its effectiveness, and help recognize your devices and serve ads. If you prefer to opt out of having your information used for interest-based advertising, you can learn how to do so by clicking here. If you would prefer to opt out of having information about your App usage used for interest-based advertising, you may do so through your device settings ("Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS and "Opt Out of Ads Personalization" on Android). Please note that if you opt out or fail to provide consent, you may continue to receive generic ads that are not based on your preferences.

Your Rights and Choices

Communication Preferences

Email, phone, and text: We may contact you (for customer support or user authentication purposes) at the email address(es) or phone number(s) provided in connection with your account. You may opt out of these messages by following the unsubscribe instructions in the message (e.g. by tapping an unsubscribe link or replying "STOP"). Please note that opting out of these messages may affect your ability to verify your account and redeem rewards, as account verification is required to claim certain benefits. If you opt out and later need to verify your account, contact Customer Support for assistance.

Push: In connection with certain Services, you may choose to opt in to receiving push notifications. If you no longer want to receive them, you can turn them off at the device level.

Cookies: Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. If you prefer, you can usually choose to set your browser to remove or reject browser cookies. Please note that if you choose to remove or reject cookies, this could affect the availability and functionality of our Services.

For more information on these rights, please refer to the additional disclosures below.

Data Retention

We may retain personal information where required by law or where we have an ongoing legitimate business need to do so. For example, we may retain such information to provide you a service you have requested, to comply with applicable legal, tax or accounting requirements, or where necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, enforcement of our contracts and terms, or for safety reasons (e.g., we may retain certain information about an account to prevent that user from opening a new account).

To protect your privacy, if you are not an active user of Bonus Round Rewards, within six months of your last activity, we delete information that is not necessary to display your App usage history, such as your location and profile information.

Security

We follow generally accepted industry standards and maintain reasonable safeguards designed to secure the information in our possession, and only those with a legitimate business need to access your personal information are permitted to do so. We also take reasonable steps to assure that third parties to whom we transfer information will provide protections appropriate to that type of information. If you have reason to believe that your interaction with us is no longer secure, please immediately contact us.

Use by Minors

We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from or about, or direct or target interest-based advertising to, anyone under the age of 16, nor do we knowingly allow such individuals to use our Services. If you believe we might have any data from or about a child under this age, please contact us. If we learn that we have inadvertently gathered such information, we will delete it.
